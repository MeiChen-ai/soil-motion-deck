# Third-party notices

## Built on

- [mathruffian-dot/soil-motion-deck](https://github.com/mathruffian-dot/soil-motion-deck) — MIT. The
  original PPTX + HyperFrames workflow; `soil-motion-html` adapts its "build once, two outputs" idea to
  HTML slide decks. The SOIL Teaching Deck Workflow (six engines) is based on Prof. 李俊儀's SOIL
  teaching method.

## Used at run time (not included — installed by the user)

- [edge-tts](https://github.com/rany2/edge-tts) — narration audio and word timings.
- [Playwright for Python](https://github.com/microsoft/playwright-python) — Apache-2.0; records the
  motion slides in the local Edge or Chrome browser.
- [imageio-ffmpeg](https://github.com/imageio/imageio-ffmpeg) — provides ffmpeg for MP4 export.
